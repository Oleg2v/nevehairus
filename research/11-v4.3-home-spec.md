# v4.3 — the home after Itzik's review (18.11.2026)

Itzik looked at the v4.2 home and answered the open questions. This spec turns his twelve points into decisions. It
amends research/10-v4.1-home-spec.md. Where they differ, this one wins.

Rules unchanged:
- test site only;
- no new plugins;
- texts from the live site, except where the client words them himself;
- no new facts;
- plain language and accessibility (IS 5568 / WCAG 2.1 AA) everywhere;
- the live site is never touched.

## 1. Hero shades

- The three shades are CSS variables on the hero, theme 1.4.0:
  - `--nh-shade-top`: under the floating header, .7;
  - `--nh-shade-ambient`: the photo's foot, .6;
  - `--nh-shade-text`: behind the text, .6.

  The defaults are 1.3.1's values.
- A photo's own values go into `templates/v4/media.json`, `hero-wide.shade` {top, ambient, text}.
  `scripts/build_v4.py` writes them into the hero's Custom CSS (`selector { --nh-shade-…: … }`), so they show and can
  be edited in Elementor.
- **Finding the values** for Itzik's photo:
  - measure the white header and hero text on the photo's pixels at 360×740, 390×844, 768×1024, 1024×768, 1280×800,
    1440×900 and 1920×1080;
  - measure at the start, middle and end of the slow zoom;
  - take the lightest shades whose minimum stays ≥4.6:1 (4.5 plus a margin).

## 5. «מעון לבעלי צרכים מיוחדים»

- **As in v4.0** (research/09 §4.5): the questions in the framed box (white, a 4px purple inline-start border, no
  shadow) and the lead are open. At their end the Nested Accordion «להמשך קריאה» (plus and minus, collapsed) opens the
  rest of the live text, which stays in the HTML for search engines. The plain twin follows.
- Why: Oleg's words «Если раньше в конце была ссылка «להמשך קריאה» на хвост текста, верни её так же». v4.0 had exactly
  that; v4.1 and v4.2 folded the whole text under it, which is what «без сворачивания под плюс» undoes.
- The live site has the whole text open and no «להמשך קריאה». `SPECIAL_READ_MORE = False` in build_v4.py gives that.
- `max_items_expended` is «multiple» (one item, so nothing else changes): with «one» and the 0 ms animation, Elementor
  4.4 closes the item right after opening it (its close-all animation finishes after the open), so «להמשך קריאה»
  never opened in v4.0–v4.2 either. Tested 18.11: click, Enter and Space open and close it.
- Once open, the region is named «להמשך קריאה»: the theme (1.4.3) points the region's aria-labelledby at the summary and
  the summary's aria-controls at the region (Elementor points both at the <details>, which named the region by its
  whole text). The opened text has no frame: it continues the paragraphs above.
- The old subline of the band below repeated the last sentence of the text. That subline is now the form's lead (§6).

## 6. The contact form (band `contact`)

- **The band:**
  - H2 «נשמח שתבואו להכיר אותנו»;
  - the lead of the inner page's form «השאירו פרטים ונחזור אליכם לתיאום מפגש היכרות.» (plain twin drafted);
  - the form in a white card, at most 640px wide, with a soft shadow.
  - No phone button: the number stays in the header and the footer.
- **The form:** Elementor Pro's own Form widget (`nh-contact-form`), not the plugin's widget.
  - It keeps the v4 rule «only Elementor's own widgets».
  - It stores submissions itself.
  - It avoids the plugin's v3 module on the home: the hero's focal point, a second cookie panel and live region.
- **The texts are #33's (plugin NH_Settings and nh-door):**
  - the required note;
  - «שם מלא» *, «טלפון» *, «משהו שחשוב לנו לדעת?» with its hint about medical details;
  - a hidden trap field;
  - the consent * (link to the privacy policy);
  - the privacy note;
  - «שליחת הבקשה»;
  - the success, error, required and invalid messages.

  Twins sit in the same field as `<span class="nh-full">` / `<span class="nh-plain">`.
- **Storage: «save-to-database» only.**
  - Submissions are listed in Elementor → Submissions.
  - A submission holds only the visitor's answers: name, phone, message, consent. The theme takes the form's HTML
    fields (the required note, the hint, the privacy note) out of the record, so the name is the list's first column.
  - `submissions_metadata` is empty, so no IP or browser is stored.
  - No e-mail is sent on the test site.
  - At launch: `LAUNCH = True` and `FORM_FROM` in build_v4.py, then `v4 --go`. That is the only path: an action added by
    hand in Elementor would use Elementor's defaults (to the WordPress admin, with the visitor's IP and browser), and the
    next `v4 --go` would drop it.
    - The e-mail goes to irus1@nevehairus.com, as plain text: «שם מלא», «טלפון», «משהו שחשוב לנו לדעת», «אישור מדיניות
      הפרטיות: כן», the date and the time. No page address, IP or browser.
    - `FORM_FROM` must be a nevehairus.com mailbox that the domain's mail accepts from the web host. nevehairus.com is on
      Microsoft 365 (SPF `include:spf.protection.outlook.com -all`, no DMARC); the host is not in the SPF. Without that,
      Microsoft 365 is likely to treat the e-mail as a spoof of its own domain and file it as Junk, while the visitor
      reads «תודה!». Before launch, agree the route with whoever runs the tenant: the host in the SPF with DKIM, or
      the mail through Microsoft 365. The build refuses `LAUNCH` without `FORM_FROM`.
    - Send one real request and read spf, dkim and compauth in the message's headers in the irus1 mailbox. With
      `FORM_FROM` set, the From is the same before and after the domain move, so a test from the host before the move
      already shows whether Microsoft 365 accepts it. It goes to the client's mailbox: only with Oleg's go-ahead.
    - The plugin's visit and jobs forms on the v3.2 pages send with WordPress's default From (wordpress@<site domain>);
      once their recipients are set at launch they need the same authorised From (a `wp_mail_from` filter).
- **Accessibility (theme, v4.js part 7):**
  - checks on send: required fields, an Israeli phone number, the consent;
  - an error summary with links, which takes the focus;
  - each error is tied to its field (aria-invalid, aria-describedby);
  - the hint is tied to the message field;
  - the result message is focused;
  - autocomplete name and tel, a left-to-right phone field;
  - 56px fields, a 3px focus ring, a 24px consent box;
  - no fade while sending.
- **On the server (functions.php):**
  - the same checks. A phone number with spaces or other dashes («050 123 4567», «050–1234567») is normalised first,
    because Elementor Pro's own check of a tel field allows only digits and ()#&+*-=.;
  - the theme's check runs after Elementor Pro's own (priority 20), so a send refused by Pro's trap is not counted;
  - at most 5 sends in a fixed 10-minute window per address, counted from the first send. Only a count and its start
    are kept, under a salted hash of the address, and a send with errors is not counted. At the limit the server
    answers at once with one message and the phone number; the visitor's typing stays in the fields. Two sends at the
    same instant may both pass (no lock): harmless at this traffic.
- **In the editor** the form's list names its text items «טקסט: שדות חובה», «טקסט: הסבר להודעה», «טקסט: פרטיות»
  (Elementor prints no label for an HTML field).

## 8. Faces

- Only the faces are blurred, everything else stays sharp.
- **Tool:** `scripts/blur_faces.py`. It applies a coarse pixelation, then a strong Gaussian blur inside an ellipse
  fitted to each face box, with a feathered edge. Copies are written without metadata.
- The originals stay in the repo history.
- The blurred files replace the media entries' files. They are uploaded as new attachments, so nothing is overwritten
  on the site.
- **Done:**
  - `card-3-v43`: all nine faces of the group photo. The home's own square pixelation left two people's ears and jaws.
  - `video-JQXm_Dku7tI-v43`: the man's face on the second video's cover; the burned-in subtitles are unchanged.
  - Nothing else on the home shows a face.
  - The YouTube video itself, once played, is not ours to change.

## 9. Cookie settings on every page

- **Our own «הגדרות עוגיות»**, moved from the plugin into the theme 1.4.0, because site-wide features belong to the
  theme.
  - The theme declares `nh-cookies`.
  - The plugin (0.2.7) prints its own panel only when the theme does not. Its widgets read and save through
    `window.nhConsent`.
  - The record is the plugin's: localStorage `nh-consent-v1` {necessary, stats, media, date, v}.
  - The version is at least 2, so everyone sees the banner once.
- **First visit:** a light banner at the foot of the screen. The buttons are «רק הכרחיות» and «אישור הכול», equally
  prominent, and «הגדרות».
  - Phones: the whole width; the floating buttons rise above it.
  - Tablets (768–1023px): at the inline start, clear of the «נגישות» button.
  - From 1024px (theme 1.4.1): at the inline end, beside the «נגישות» button, so the hero's title, subline and both
    buttons stay in view.
  - The page keeps room for it at its end. Keyboard focus is never left under it: a focused control behind it is
    scrolled just above it (or, if taller than the room, its top comes under the header).
- **By default only necessary storage.**
- **Where the browser blocks site data** (localStorage throws), no banner: nothing could be kept, it would come back on
  every page, and a video still asks in place before YouTube loads. A choice made there holds for the page view.
- **The version** is the plugin's «גרסת ההסכמה» + 1 (at least 2), so every raise of the setting asks everyone again; the
  plugin's own check (record version ≥ the setting) accepts the theme's records.
- **Zoom and large text:** on screens up to 767px the floating buttons rise above the banner but never above the
  header; on a short screen (≤500px high: browser zoom 200–400%) and with the panel's largest text the banner stands
  beside them instead, and while the banner is shown «נגישות» opens under the header.
- **«הגדרות עוגיות»** in both footers opens the settings: necessary (always) and embedded content (YouTube), with
  «שמירת הבחירה» and «סגירה». Esc and focus return work.
- **YouTube loads only after a click.** While embedded content is not allowed, the click asks in place first, with the
  question of the plugin's voices widget. «כן» saves the choice and plays; «לא עכשיו» goes back to the play button.
  Then the player takes the focus. Once embedded content is allowed, Enter or Space on a cover plays the video (Space no
  longer scrolls the page) and the player takes the focus too. A key held down does not answer the question: «כן» needs
  its own press.
- **Texts:** the plugin's settings (cookie_*), with the same defaults in the theme. They are still marked for the
  lawyer's review (research/07).
