## 1. Hero shades

- The three shades are CSS variables on the hero, theme 1.4.0:
  - `--nh-shade-top`: under the floating header, .7;
  - `--nh-shade-ambient`: the photo's foot, .6;
  - `--nh-shade-text`: behind the text, .6.

  The defaults are 1.3.1's values.
- A photo's own values go into `templates/v4/media.json`, `hero-wide.shade` {top, ambient, text}.
  `scripts/build_v4.py` writes them into the hero's Custom CSS (`selector { --nh-shade-…: … }`), so they show and can
  be edited in Elementor.
- **Finding the values** for Itzik's photo:
  - measure the white header and hero text on the photo's pixels at 360×740, 390×844, 768×1024, 1024×768, 1280×800,
    1440×900 and 1920×1080;
  - measure at the start, middle and end of the slow zoom;
  - take the lightest shades whose minimum stays ≥4.6:1 (4.5 plus a margin).

## 3. «הבית שלנו» (section `specialties`)

- H2 «הבית שלנו», the client's words. It replaces the live «תחומי התמחות», which is kept as a retired block.
- **The four cards:**
  - white, radius 16px;
  - a soft shadow from Elementor's own Box Shadow: 0 8px 24px −4px rgba(28,24,48,.14);
  - no line;
  - on hover or keyboard focus the shadow deepens and the card lifts, as before;
  - 4 / 2 / 2 columns, the same as v4.2.
- **Then a visually hidden H3 «סרטונים» and the two videos:**
  - each a white card with the same shadow;
  - the video fills the card's top, the caption sits under it with padding;
  - 2 / 2 / 1 columns;
  - lazy loading, privacy mode, our own thumbnails: nothing from YouTube before a click.
- The separate videos section is gone.

## 7. One cream ground

- **Kit colour «קרם» #F7F1E7.** This is the inner page's paper.
- **Every section uses it:** «הבית שלנו», «החיים בנווה האירוס», «מקצועיות», «חדר סנוזלן», «מעון לבעלי צרכים
  מיוחדים», «נשמח שתבואו להכיר אותנו». The page ground under them is the same colour. The hero keeps its photo.
- **No sky, lilac or green grounds.** The framed box of «מעון לבעלי צרכים מיוחדים» is white with its purple border.
- **The footer stays night #1C1830.**
- **Contrast on cream:**

  | Text | Contrast |
  |---|---|
  | ink #1F2421 | 14.0:1 |
  | ink-2 #4F5550 | 6.8:1 |
  | purple-700 #4A2D84 | 9.35:1 |
  | purple-800 #3A2269 | 11.6:1 |

  Links are purple and underlined.

## 8. Faces

- Only the faces are blurred, everything else stays sharp.
- **Tool:** `scripts/blur_faces.py`. It applies a coarse pixelation, then a strong Gaussian blur inside an ellipse
  fitted to each face box, with a feathered edge. Copies are written without metadata.
- The originals stay in the repo history.
- The blurred files replace the media entries' files. They are uploaded as new attachments, so nothing is overwritten
  on the site.

## 12. WhatsApp

- The build check refuses any `wa.me` link.
