Itzik looked at v4.3: «стало гораздо лучше», and sent seven points. This spec amends
research/11-v4.3-home-spec.md. Where they differ, this one wins.

The rules are unchanged:
- test site only;
- no new plugins;
- texts from the live site;
- no new facts;
- plain language and accessibility (IS 5568 / WCAG 2.1 AA) everywhere;
- the live site is never touched.

## 1. One rhythm

- Every section has the same padding above and below, `SECTION_PAD` in `scripts/build_v4.py`:
  - 3.5rem on desktops;
  - 3rem on tablets;
  - 2.5rem on phones.
- So the space between two sections is 112 / 96 / 80 px at every width.
- Before, it was 160 between most sections, and 80, 101 or 128 next to the life photo, which had no padding of its own.
- The hero is a full-bleed photo. Below it, the next section's own padding is the gap: 56 / 48 / 40 px.
- The measured result: at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 360 px, each section's first and last visible thing sits exactly
  one padding from its edge.

## 5. Hero: Itzik's new photo

- The photo came only as a phone screenshot of his e-mail: about 1180 px wide, its edge under the mail's toolbar.
  That is too small for the first screen (a 1440 px screen at 2× needs about 2880 px). It goes in once the original
  file arrives.
- **Phones:** cropped to a phone screen, the new photo keeps both the sky ceiling and the door at the end of the
  corridor. So one photo serves every width, and the vertical photo is not needed.
- **The shades' reach** became CSS variables on the hero (theme 1.4.5), beside the three strengths of 1.4.0. They are
  lengths in px, rem or em:
  - `--nh-shade-top-hold`: how far the top shade holds below the header (default 1rem);
  - `--nh-shade-top-fade`: how long it then fades (4rem);
  - `--nh-shade-top-min`: its least height (160px);
  - `--nh-shade-text-rise`: how far above the text the text shade begins (8rem);
  - `--nh-shade-text-ramp`: how long it fades in (6rem).

  The defaults are 1.4.4's values, so the current hero looks the same. A photo's own values come from
  `templates/v4/media.json` `shade` (`top_hold`, `top_fade`, `top_min`, `text_rise`, `text_ramp`), written by the
  builder into the hero's Custom CSS.
- **Values for the new photo**, tuned on the screenshot by the method of research/11 §1:
  - 7 screen sizes × the start, middle and end of the slow zoom;
  - the white text's darkest background pixel, with the text hidden.

  ```json
  {"top": 0.63, "ambient": 0, "text": 0.62, "top_hold": "0px", "top_fade": "1.5rem", "top_min": "0px",
   "text_rise": "4rem", "text_ramp": "3rem"}
  ```

  - The top shade covers the header only and ends 1.5rem below it (was 5rem, at least 160 px); .63 instead of .7.
  - No shade over the lower 70% of the photo (was up to .6 at the foot).
  - Only a band behind the text, .62, beginning 4rem above it (was 8rem).
  - Measured minimums: the menu and header 5.05:1, the hero's subtitle 4.88:1.
- **When the original arrives:**
  1. Save it to `templates/v4/media/` at about 2560 px wide, without metadata; look for faces (none expected).
  2. Add its `media.json` entry with alt, focus and the shade above.
  3. Set `HERO_PHOTO` in the builder, build, render-check, `v4 --go`.
  4. Measure again on the real pixels at 7 sizes × 3 zoom states, keeping ≥4.6:1.
  5. Soften the shades' edges where the photo allows. On the bright walls the end of the top shade and the start of
     the text band show as soft lines.
