# v4.4 — the home after Itzik's second look (19.11.2026)

Itzik looked at v4.3: «стало гораздо лучше», and sent seven points. This spec amends
research/11-v4.3-home-spec.md. Where they differ, this one wins.

The rules are unchanged:
- test site only;
- no new plugins;
- texts from the live site;
- no new facts;
- plain language and accessibility (IS 5568 / WCAG 2.1 AA) everywhere;
- the live site is never touched.

## 1. One rhythm

- Every section has the same padding above and below, `SECTION_PAD` in `scripts/build_v4.py`:
  - 3.5rem on desktops;
  - 3rem on tablets;
  - 2.5rem on phones.
- So the space between two sections is 112 / 96 / 80 px at every width.
- Before, it was 160 between most sections, and 80, 101 or 128 next to the life photo, which had no padding of its own.
- The hero is a full-bleed photo. Below it, the next section's own padding is the gap: 56 / 48 / 40 px.
- The measured result: at 1440, 1024, 768, 390 and 360 px, each section's first and last visible thing sits exactly
  one padding from its edge.

## 5. Hero: Itzik's new photo

- **The file.** Oleg sent it on 19.11: a PNG of 1717x916 with a 256-colour palette and no metadata, so not the
  photographer's original. The e-mail's screenshot before it was 1179 px wide, its edge under the mail's toolbar.
- **The asset** is `templates/v4/media/hero-corridor.jpg` (media.json `hero-corridor`, attachment #124):
  - the palette's dither grain cleaned with ImageMagick `-selective-blur 0x1.6+7%`;
  - enlarged to 2560 px wide with Lanczos and a light unsharp mask;
  - JPEG q86, no metadata.

  Real detail stays that of 1717x916. On desktops it is about as sharp as the old hero (1.0 image px per CSS px at
  1440x900, the old one 1.25); on phones it is softer (about 0.46 real px per device px on an iPhone 13). The old one,
  3024x4032, was sharp there. Replace it with the original when it comes: one file, the rest stays.
- **No faces:** through the glass door only trees, planters and a building.
- **Phones.** Cropped to a phone screen, the photo keeps both the sky ceiling and the door at the end of the
  corridor. So one photo serves every width (focus 50% 40%), and the vertical photo is not needed.
- **`sizes` (theme 1.4.7, `nh_child_hero_sizes`).**
  - The photo is landscape and fills the screen by object-fit: cover. Wherever the screen is narrower than the photo's
    shape (phones held upright, 1440x900, 1920x1080), it is shown by the screen's height.
  - WordPress's `sizes` (100vw) made phones pick the 1536 px file, 820 px high.
  - For attachments titled `nh4:hero-…` the theme now sets
    `(max-aspect-ratio: W/H) calc(100vh * W/H), 100vw`.
  - Measured: iPhone 13, iPhone SE, Pixel 7, iPad Mini and 1440x900@2 load the 2560 px file; 1920x1080@1 loads 2048;
    1366x768@1 loads 1536.
  - Portrait photos keep WordPress's value.
- **The shades' reach** became CSS variables on the hero (theme 1.4.5), beside the three strengths of 1.4.0. They are
  lengths in px, rem or em:
  - `--nh-shade-top-hold`: how far the top shade holds below the header (default 1rem);
  - `--nh-shade-top-fade`: how long it then fades (4rem);
  - `--nh-shade-top-min`: its least height (160px);
  - `--nh-shade-text-rise`: how far above the text the text shade begins (8rem);
  - `--nh-shade-text-ramp`: how long it fades in (6rem).

  A photo's own values come from `templates/v4/media.json` `shade` (`top_hold`, `top_fade`, `top_min`, `text_rise`,
  `text_ramp`). The builder writes them into the hero's Custom CSS.
- **Eased edges (1.4.7).** The top shade fades out along the «scrim» curve: 74% at 19% of the way, 38% at 47%, 8% at
  80%, 2% at 91%. The text band fades in along the same curve upside down. So neither ends in a line on a white wall
  or a bright floor.
- **A bug fixed (1.4.8).** The theme's defaults (`.elementor-element.e-con.nh-hero:has(.nh-hero__img)`, 0,4,0)
  outweighed the hero's Custom CSS (`.elementor-32 .elementor-element.elementor-element-<id>`, 0,3,0). So from 1.4.0
  on, a photo's own values were never applied. In v4.3 no photo had any, so nothing showed. The defaults are now in
  `:where(…)`, with no specificity.
- **Values, measured on the deployed page** (`herolive.mjs`: 7 screens × the start, middle and end of the slow zoom;
  the white text's lightest background pixel, with the text hidden):

  ```json
  {"top": 0.63, "ambient": 0, "text": 0.62, "top_hold": "0px", "top_fade": "2rem", "top_min": "0px",
   "text_rise": "5rem", "text_ramp": "4rem"}
  ```

  - The top shade covers the header and ends 2rem below it (was 5rem, at least 160 px); .63 instead of .7.
  - No shade over the lower 70% of the photo (was up to .6 at the foot).
  - Only a band behind the text, .62, beginning 5rem above it (was 8rem).
  - Minimums: the menu and header 5.05:1, the hero's text 4.88:1.
  - Lighter fails: there are white pixels under the letters. Text .58 gives 4.30:1, top .60 gives 4.57:1.
